# Changelog

## 0.1.0
- Initial release.
- REI: the "+" button moves crafting recipes into the OmniVault crafting terminal (and wireless terminal), taking items from the network and your inventory.
- REI: any recipe can be loaded into the Pattern Encoding Terminal; crafting recipes keep their shape, everything else becomes a processing pattern.
- REI and JEI: hover an item or fluid in a terminal's network grid and press your recipe/usage keys to look it up.
- REI and JEI: the overlays stay clear of the terminal's side buttons.
- REI: OmniVault terminals are listed as crafting workstations.
- EMI support will follow once EMI is released for Minecraft 26.x.
